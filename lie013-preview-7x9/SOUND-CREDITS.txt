# WILMACHORD V0.2 sound design and provenance

The twelve sounds use seven rendering paths. Effects are shared downstream; changing only effects is not how this version creates its different instruments. All six V0.1 preset IDs remain valid. Some sound characters deliberately change to address the V0.1 feedback.

| Instrument | Source / architecture |
| --- | --- |
| MOTORIK | Drawbar sine ranks with pulse modulation locked to the musical clock |
| KOSMISCHE | PolyBLEP saw/pulse sources, a moving resonant state-variable filter, sub-octave and ring modulation |
| HYPNOSIS | Three detuned drawbar ranks, sub-octave and slow ensemble modulation |
| HAUNTED | Struck-reed modal synthesis with independently decaying, slightly inharmonic resonances |
| ROCKSTEADY | Hollow drawbar voicing, decaying third-harmonic percussion and key click |
| CASSETTE | Cascaded FM electric-tine synthesis, decaying modulation and tremolo |
| TAPE STRINGS | Six recorded VSCO 2 CE violin-ensemble pitch zones, with tape bandwidth, saturation and pitch instability |
| TAPE CHOIR | Six FreePats Synth Pad Choir pitch zones, with tape treatment. These are samples of a ZynAddSubFX synthetic choir, not recorded singers |
| GRAND | Fourteen VCSL Kawai grand recordings: seven pitch zones, two velocity layers |
| SCHOOL PIANO | Twelve VCSL Knight upright recordings: six pitch zones, two velocity layers |
| HONKY TONK | The upright bank with three independently detuned sample voices, rather than an effect applied to GRAND |
| GARAGE | Band-limited pulse-divider transistor-organ synthesis and asymmetric saturation |

These are lightweight, characterful browser instruments. The tape instruments do **not** use authentic Mellotron tape recordings and are not claimed to be exact reproductions. The piano banks contain real piano recordings, but omit full commercial-library features such as damper resonance, release samples and many round robins. Original synthetic instruments are not claimed to be sampled acoustic instruments.

## Bundled recordings

All source recordings are CC0. Complete licence texts and original publisher notes are in `docs/licenses/`.

- Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), Versilian Studios: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL at `c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e`.
- VSCO 2 Community Edition, Versilian Studios: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE at `440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e`.
- FreePats Synth Pad Choir (2020-05-16), samples by 快乐爱的小精灵 (Elf of Happy and Love), edited by Roberto: https://github.com/freepats/synth-pad-choir at `b801f7fc3eee44f575cd480750aea4f6a16a07a9`.

`src/audio/manifest.json` records every upstream path, pinned repository commit, source SHA-256, prepared WAV SHA-256, pitch, velocity layer and loop point. VCSL/VSCO filenames use an octave convention one below scientific pitch notation; e.g. their A3 recording is A4/MIDI 69. The FreePats SFZ uses scientific C4/MIDI 60. Representative fundamentals were checked before mapping.

Preparation: convert to 32 kHz, 16-bit mono PCM with CoreAudio sample-rate conversion; trim microphone lead-in while retaining attacks; normalize each zone to 0.82 peak; prepare a 180 ms sustain crossfade. Grand/upright clips are up to 6 s, strings 5 s, choir 6.79 s. Original pitch and timbre differences between piano velocity layers remain; the renderer crossfades them by gesture velocity. Loop endpoints join consecutive samples from the same source. Mono samples are gently panned per note into the stereo effects bus.

The choir FLAC originals were decoded with the MIT-licensed `@wasm-audio-decoders/flac` 0.2.11 preparation tool from the official npm registry. This tool is **not** a runtime dependency and is not redistributed. Final WAV build inputs are included, so rebuilding never requires FLAC decoding or downloading anything.

`tools/prepare-samples.py` is an optional macOS preparation script for already-downloaded/decoded source WAVs and their manifest. Normal builds use `tools/embed-samples.py`, which reads the included WAVs using Python's standard library. The resulting PCM bank is embedded directly in the HTML. No audio fetches, codec downloads or external sample servers are used while playing.

## Performance, sustain and safety

The bank contains 38 zones, about 14.3 MB PCM / 28.6 MB decoded Float32 data. Decode and transfer happen once after TOUCH TO PLAY, before the overlay closes. The AudioWorklet owns the sample data, 32 primary voices and short fading voice-steal tails. Native system audio latency still depends on the device and output route; Bluetooth adds latency.

PLUCK uses a decaying excitation/envelope; sampled pianos play their recording's decay. HOLD keeps a pointer-owned voice alive, using crossfaded sample loops where required. Piano HOLD intentionally sustains the quiet sampled tail rather than pretending a real piano sustains forever. Finger release fades the note; loops continue through release so the recording cannot end abruptly midway through the envelope. Switching string count or mode releases all held strings. Extreme high voicings fold down whole octaves to stay at or below MIDI 120.

The output includes a DC blocker, smooth gain control and a soft limiter. CRUSH deliberately creates distortion and stepped waveforms. Automated finite-output and envelope checks do not establish that a listener will never hear a click or dislike a timbre. Per-instrument and snare WAV auditions are included for listening review. Physical iPhone performance and subjective listening remain acceptance checks.

Six snares use different shell pitches, wire/noise filtering, excitation and decay structures: dry acoustic-style, vintage machine, tight 60s, resonant dub, quantized lo-fi and electronic noise. They are synthesized, not recordings. Selection is independent of instruments and patterns; a new selection applies on the next snare hit.
