Shop Information

Shipping

Prints are currently available for delivery within the UK.

All prints are supplied unframed and are carefully packaged for delivery.

Returns

If you change your mind about an order, please email within 14 days of receiving it.

You then have 14 days to return the print. Prints should be returned in their original condition and packaging.

Return postage is the responsibility of the customer unless the item is faulty, damaged or incorrect.

If your order arrives damaged or there is a problem with it, please get in touch and I will sort it out.

Contact

For questions about an order, shipping or returns:

studio@christytaylor.co.uk

Privacy

Personal information supplied when placing an order is used to process and fulfil your order, communicate with you about it and meet any legal or accounting requirements.

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. I do not receive or store your full card details.

Order information may be retained where necessary for accounting, tax or legal purposes and is not sold to third parties.

For questions about your personal information, contact studio@christytaylor.co.uk .
